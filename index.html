Three Types of Japanese Adverbs
副詞の三分類

Japanese adverbs are traditionally divided into three categories based on what they modify and what grammatical constraints they carry. Understanding the type helps you use them naturally and avoid errors.

Type 01
情態の副詞
Manner Adverbs

Describe how an action is performed. Modify verbs and adjectives. No specific grammatical constraints on what follows — they just describe the manner or style of the action.

ゆっくり はっきり きちんと どんどん
Type 02
程度の副詞
Degree Adverbs

Describe how much / how intense something is. Typically modify adjectives and adjectival verbs. Show the scale or degree of a quality or state.

とても 少し かなり もっと
Type 03
呼応の副詞
Correlative Adverbs

Must pair with a specific predicate ending. The adverb "calls" and the sentence ending "responds." Using the wrong ending is a grammar error. Also called 陳述の副詞.

ぜんぜん たぶん もし たとえ
01 —
情態の副詞
Manner Adverbs — "how" something is done

These adverbs modify verbs or adjectives to describe the manner, state, or style of an action. Many are mimetic words (擬態語). They are the most flexible type — no special grammatical constraints on the predicate.

💡
Key feature
情態の副詞 often end in 〜り (ゆっくり、はっきり) or 〜と (きちんと、しっかりと). Many overlap with mimetic words (オノマトペ). They freely modify any verb without constraints.

02 —
程度の副詞
Degree Adverbs — "how much / how intense"

These adverbs show the degree or intensity of an adjective or verb. They sit on a scale from very low (ほとんど, 少し) to very high (とても, 非常に). Many pair naturally with both positive and negative predicates.

📊
Intensity scale
High → 非常に > すごく > とても > かなり > だいぶ > けっこう
Low → 少し > ちょっと > あまり（＋否定）> ほとんど（＋否定）

03 —
呼応の副詞
Correlative Adverbs — must "call and respond"

Also called 陳述の副詞. These adverbs require a specific predicate ending. The adverb triggers a grammatical obligation. Using the wrong ending is incorrect Japanese — this is a very common JLPT exam point.

⚠️
JLPT Exam Alert
呼応の副詞 are frequently tested in N4–N3 grammar questions. A typical question shows the adverb and asks you to choose the correct sentence ending. Memorise the pairs — not just the adverbs alone.

📝
Exam tip — spotting the type quickly
If the adverb ends in 〜り / 〜と / 〜に and describes a motion or state → likely 情態.
If it can be replaced by "very" or "a little" → likely 程度.
If removing the sentence ending makes the sentence feel broken or wrong → definitely 呼応.
